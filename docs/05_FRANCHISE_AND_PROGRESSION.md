# FRANCHISE & PROGRESSION

## Fantasy

Start with a small, rough club and build the greatest show in baseball. Growth is visible in roster quality, attendance, fame, stadium, presentation, sponsors, rivalries, and the sophistication of the team's show.

## Strategic Axes

### Baseball

Roster construction, lineup, rotation, bullpen, development, fatigue, injuries, tactics.

### Business

Payroll, tickets, sponsors, merchandise, facilities, stadium improvements, promotion.

### Entertainment

Charisma, signature tricks, celebrations, events, rivalries, crowd culture, show identity.

## Calendar

Training, rest, medical, promotion/community appearances, team events, rivalry events, sponsor obligations, and games. Keep calendar decisions meaningful rather than filling every day with chores.

## Player Development

Players improve through play, training, coaching, role, and events. Growth can unlock signature moves and traits, not only numeric ratings. Aging, fatigue, and injury create roster turnover without becoming a medical simulator.

## Relationships

Friendships, mentorships, grudges, leadership, cliques, and rivalries create event hooks and limited gameplay effects. Keep state simple enough to explain.

## Fame

Individual fame and franchise fame are separate. A mediocre but charismatic fan favorite can remain commercially valuable. Fame unlocks events, sponsor tiers, entrances, portrait treatments, merchandise, and league attention.

## Sponsors

Sponsors issue optional objectives that create interesting tension: start a fan favorite, attempt Show Plays, feature a rookie, win by a threshold, promote a product. Rewards should tempt, not dictate.

## Stadium Growth

Progress from improvised local park to iconic spectacle venue. Upgrades can improve revenue, crowd retention, training, recovery, presentation, or unlock stadium-specific entertainment systems.

## History

Persist championships, records, retired players, rivalries, signature moments, awards, career totals, and franchise milestones. The save file should become a story archive.

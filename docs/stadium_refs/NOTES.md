# Stadium Reference Notes

What each owner-supplied reference sheet shows that the parks don't have
yet, and what that means for the modules and the shared core. These are
reference only: nothing from the images ships in the game. Field
dimensions stay as tuned (`FieldShape`); where a sheet disagrees, the
rules win.

## 01 The Halo Dome (`01_halo_dome.jpg`)

- **Stands:** three steep decks, each with a glowing fascia band carrying team wordmarks ("GLITTERATI", "NEON HEIGHTS"). We have one sloped bowl.
- **Center field:** a framed video board with the team logo, flanked by pylons, waterfalls either side, palm trees.
- **Halo:** pylons hung with vertical logo banners (we have plain pylons); airships in the sky.
- **Scoreboard:** batter portrait and number, AVG/HR/RBI, ball/strike/out dots, a base diamond (we have line score, count lamps and the batter's name).
- **Wall:** dark padded panels with logo and wordmark panels, distances on plaques, yellow foul poles.
- **Dugouts:** lit interiors with bat racks, helmet shelves and the club's logo on the back wall; each in its club's colors.
- **Press box:** a glass announcer's booth behind home with the logo above.
- **Identity:** a team emblem (halo ring and star) used everywhere.

## 02 Foundry Field (`02_foundry_field.jpg`)

- **Industry:** smokestacks with smoke, cranes and steel lattice towers over and behind the stands; a river with a bridge beyond.
- **Stands:** decks under riveted truss canopies, catwalks, brick and iron facades.
- **Center field:** an arched iron gate with the gear logo and "FOUNDRY FIELD"; a green hedge batter's eye; waterfalls.
- **Scoreboard:** an iron frame with gear-and-wrench crests, the same stat layout as the Halo Dome.
- **Wall:** dark panels with wordmarks. The sheet shows a low wall in right, but the Boilerplate (34 ft iron wall) stays as tuned.
- **Identity:** gear-and-wrench emblem; orange, rust, charcoal and cream palette.

## Shared across the sheets (so far)

The same set of views repeats, which points to shared pieces rather than one-offs:

- **Core changes:**
  - **Decks:** tiered stands with fascia bands instead of one bowl. Settings: decks, rows per deck, overhang, canopy.
  - **Team emblem:** a simple vector emblem per club (shape and colors), reused on boards, walls, dugouts, flags and the press box. The team designer needs it.
  - **Richer scoreboard:** player stats and a base diamond.
  - **Dugouts in the club's style:** bat rack, helmets, logo wall.
- **New modules:**
  - centerpiece (video board, gate or arch);
  - waterfalls;
  - trees (palms, pines, cacti);
  - airships;
  - smokestacks with smoke;
  - cranes and lattice towers;
  - truss canopy;
  - press box;
  - banner flagpoles;
  - wall ad panels;
  - river and bridge (a bay variant).

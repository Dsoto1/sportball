# Stadium Reference Notes

What the owner-supplied reference sheets show and what that means for the
shared core and the modules. Reference only: nothing from the images ships
in the game. Field dimensions stay as tuned (`FieldShape`). The distances
on the sheets are illustrative; where a sheet disagrees, the rules win.

## The sheet format (v2)

Every sheet has the same 11 views:

1. Establishing view from behind home.
2. Home plate close-up.
3. The pitcher's view.
4. Home dugout on the first-base line.
5. Visitor dugout on the third-base line.
6. Left-field corner.
7. Left-center.
8. Center field.
9. Right-center.
10. Right-field corner.
11. Aerial overview.

Below them, an outfield wall strip from pole to pole and a list of key
points. The Foundry sheet also has a six-color team set.

## Rules the sheets set (all parks)

- **Foul poles:** yellow, at the corners where the wall meets the lines.
- **Wall:** continuous from pole to pole, the same height everywhere unless a park's shape says otherwise. No outfield dugouts, benches or field-level seating.
- **Everything else is behind the wall:** seating, scenery, waterfalls and architecture, never at field level.
- **Dugouts:** two only, home on the first-base side and visitors on the third-base side, near home plate. The home dugout is in club colors with its wordmark; the visitors' says "VISITORS". The game now builds them this way.
- **Wall dressing:** the emblem near each pole and in the alleys, distances in big gold numbers on the wall.

## Shared structure (same on both sheets)

- **Centerpiece:** the main scoreboard moves to straightaway center, above the batter's eye. It is crowned with the emblem and shows the club name, the batter's portrait, number, name, position and AVG/HR/RBI. Ours sits in right-center.
- **Light towers:** tall towers rising out of the upper stands, a pair flanking the centerpiece and more along the lines.
- **Slogan boards:** big signs over the right-field stands with the park name and the club slogan ("NEON HEIGHTS / Bigger Brighter Together", "FOUNDRY FIELD / Built Harder Together").
- **Stands:** two to three tiers wrapping the park, with wordmark fascia bands and emblem banners on the pillars.
- **Beyond the outfield:** themed scenery in the gaps between the centerpiece and the towers.

## What to build

- **Core:**
  - tiered decks with fascia bands;
  - yellow foul poles;
  - wall emblem panels and big distance numbers;
  - dugouts as above (done).
- **Team identity:**
  - a six-role color set (primary, secondary, accent, support, dark, highlight), as on the Foundry sheet;
  - an emblem;
  - a slogan;
  - all three feed the park's palette and signs.
- **Modules:**
  - centerpiece scoreboard (in center, with an optional crown such as the halo);
  - slogan board;
  - light towers rising from the stands;
  - waterfalls;
  - trees;
  - flying craft;
  - smokestacks with smoke;
  - cranes and gantries;
  - river and bridge;
  - furnace glow.

# DECISIONS

## 2026-10-20 - Engine and Language

Decision: Godot 4.7.2 stable + GDScript for the initial PC build.

Reason: Strong fit for stylized 3D plus heavy UI, fast iteration, open-source tooling and manageable indie/agent-assisted development.

## 2026-10-20 - Authoritative Game State

Decision: The 3D scene is not authoritative game state.

Reason: Supports testability, AI, replays, simulation, save/load, franchise statistics, debugging and possible future networking.

## 2026-10-20 - Asset Strategy

Decision: Free/CC0-first. Free assets are raw material and must be normalized to the game's art direction.

Reason: Reduce cost/production burden without an asset-flip identity.

## 2026-10-20 - Asset Provenance

Decision: Every third-party production asset requires a source/license entry in 13_ASSET_MANIFEST.md.

Reason: "Free" does not guarantee commercial/redistribution rights; release provenance must be auditable.

## 2026-10-20 - Phase 0 Field Coordinates and Units

Decision: Meters, seconds, degrees. Home plate at the origin, +Y up, center field toward -Z, first-base side toward +X. Spray angle 0 = straight away center, positive toward right field; foul beyond ±45°.

Reason: One convention shared by rules, math, presentation and tests.

Affected systems: all baseball services, presentation, tuning data.

## 2026-10-20 - Ball In Play Is Resolved in Two Stages

Decision: `BallInPlay.create()` computes trajectory, the first fielder to reach the ball, and every runner's committed target. `resolve(throw_base)` then applies a single throw and the force/tag/third-out rules. Runners commit at contact and do not change their minds mid-play.

Reason: Keeps the outcome fully authoritative and testable while still leaving a real-time decision (the throw) to a human defender.

Affected systems: fielding, base running, AI, MatchDirector. Superseded in part by "Multi-Throw Plays" (the single throw became a chain).

## 2026-10-20 - Fielder Routes Are Automatic in Phase 0 (superseded)

Decision: Fielders take the optimal intercept route; the human chooses the throw target.

Superseded the same day by "Human Steers the Fielder".

## 2026-10-20 - Human Steers the Fielder

Decision: On defense the player steers one fielder (initially whoever the projection says reaches the ball first; `switch_fielder` hands control to the fielder nearest the ball). `LiveFielding` steps all fielders at the fixed sim step from movement intents: the controlled fielder follows the stick, AI teammates run projected routes. Catching is automatic when the ball is within reach and low enough; `dive` extends reach once, and a missed dive leaves the fielder down for `dive_recovery`. While a fielder is controlled, AI teammates cannot catch on the fly (they only back up), so the player's route decides fly balls. Runners re-read the play from the actual fielding result. A human may hold the ball up to 0.6 s before the AI throw is used; holding delays the throw. A landing-spot ring shows where a fly will come down.

Reason: Owner decision. Keeps defense skill-based ("do not automate the fun out of defense") while trajectory, catch, throw and base rules stay authoritative and deterministic for a given input sequence.

Affected systems: fielding, BallInPlay, input (`dive`, `switch_fielder`), MatchDirector, MatchView, playtest bot. AI-vs-AI simulation still uses the instant projection (equivalent to all-AI LiveFielding; covered by tests).

## 2026-10-20 - Input Map Generated From a Table

Decision: Semantic actions are stored in `project.godot` but generated by `tools/generate_input_map.gd`. Actions not used yet (`show_modifier`, `runner_advance`, `runner_retreat`) are not defined until a system uses them.

Reason: One reviewable binding table; avoids empty architecture.

Affected systems: input, UI prompts.

## 2026-10-20 - No Autoloads and No Third-Party Test Framework

Decision: Content is loaded by `ContentLibrary` and passed explicitly. Tests use a ~60-line in-repo runner instead of GUT.

Reason: Autoloads are not needed yet; adding a third-party addon would require manifest review for little Phase 0 benefit.

Affected systems: bootstrap, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Phase 0 Simplifications

Decision: Right-handed placeholder batters only; anonymous placeholder lineups (`away_01` ...); no Signature pitch family, bunts, steals, Show modifier or errors; swing timing is sampled at frame granularity; a walk-off scores only the winning run unless it is a home run; ties continue to extra innings.

Reason: Keep Phase 0 to the core loop. Each is a small, reversible addition.

Affected systems: batting, pitching, MatchState.

## 2026-10-20 - No Mobile Optimization

Decision: Do not optimize for mobile hardware or touch input. Target desktop (Windows PC first) with the Forward+ renderer; scalable quality tiers apply to modest desktop hardware only.

Reason: Owner direction. Mobile constraints would limit rendering, crowd density and control design without serving the target platform.

Affected systems: rendering, performance budgets, input, UI layout.

## 2026-10-20 - Timing-First Batting

Decision: The batter no longer aims a cursor. `SwingIntent` carries swing type, timing offset and the stick held at the swing. Timing sets contact quality and pull/push; pitch distance from the heart of the zone costs quality (worse for power swings), and pitches beyond `reach_limit` whiff. The stick adds up to ±15° of spray (absolute field direction) and ±20° of lift/chop. Mishits widen the launch and spray scatter. The pitcher keeps the aim cursor.

Reason: Owner decision. Simpler to learn ("within 2 minutes, understand how to hit") while timing, take/swing discipline and situational stick input remain skill.

Affected systems: ContactResolver, SwingIntent, ContactResult, AI batter, BaseballTuning (batting group), MatchDirector input/HUD, tests. Batting windows were retuned against the AI sim (contact ±78 ms, power ±52 ms).

## 2026-10-20 - Event-Stamped Swing Timing

Decision: Human swings are captured in `MatchDirector._input` and stamped with `Time.get_ticks_usec()` on delivery instead of being polled in `_process`. `SwingTiming.offset()` subtracts half the last frame interval (capped at 1/30 s), because a press is delivered at the start of the frame after it happens. Pitch flight is drawn from the same microsecond clock, so what is seen and what is judged cannot drift (delta smoothing and hitches no longer matter). Tools that run faster than real time (`--fixed-fps`) set `use_wall_clock = false` and use accumulated frame time instead. `swing_latency_calibration` (tuning) holds a future per-player display-latency offset.

Reason: Per-frame polling read every swing 0–1 frame late (bias ~8 ms, worst ~17 ms at 60 fps), about a third of the ±52 ms power window. The new path is unbiased with ±½ frame jitter. Godot 4.7.2 input events carry no timestamps, so this is the floor without a native input extension.

Affected systems: MatchDirector (input, pitch flight clock), new `app/swing_timing.gd`, BaseballTuning, playtest bot (sends real InputEventAction for swings), tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Multi-Throw Plays

Decision: After the ball is secured, `ThrowPlay` resolves the defense one leg at a time, up to 3 legs. Each leg goes to a base. The first runner heading there is out if the ball beats them, with a tag required unless the force still holds. The receiver can throw again after `relay_release_time`, plus tag time if they just tagged. A fielder within `unassisted_distance` of the target base takes it there himself. A force holds only while every runner behind in the force chain is alive, so after the batter is out at first, second needs a tag. The third-out rules apply to the third out in time order. Runners still commit when the ball is read and do not react to throws.

The AI picks each leg by trying every base on a copy of the play with one throw of look-ahead, scoring outs against runs with a small preference for retiring lead runners. Humans press base buttons; presses are buffered, so a double play can be called before the catch. With no input, the AI throw is used after the decision window. Outcomes gain `double_play` and `triple_play` kinds and a `throws` list.

Reason: Double plays and relays are core baseball. The leg-by-leg model keeps the human in the decision while the outcome stays authoritative and testable.

Affected systems: superseded the same day by "Live Runners and Rundowns" (ThrowPlay and AiDefense were removed).

## 2026-10-20 - Live Runners and Rundowns

Decision: Everything after contact except the batted ball runs in `LivePlay`, a fixed-step simulation of runners (position along the base path), the ball once fielded, throws between base covers, the ball holder's movement, and covers closing in. Outs come from positions and the rules:
- **Force:** the holder is on the bag before a forced runner gets there. A force holds only while every runner behind is alive, and a forced runner who stays put is still liable.
- **Tag:** the holder is within `tag_reach` of a runner who is off a bag.
- **Doubled off:** the ball beats a runner back to a base he had not retouched after a catch.

Ties go to the runner. Third-out run rules are applied by time.

Runner behaviour:
- Runners start from the read (`RunnerResolver.plan`), freeze on a ball in the air (unless there are two outs), and retouch after a catch.
- Standing runners take a base on a throw elsewhere, or tag up, only with `runner_take_base_margin`.
- A runner who would lose the race reverses after `runner_reverse_time` if that is better. This is how rundowns emerge.
- With the ball held and no force or tag available, the defense AI chases a nearby runner off a bag; otherwise the fielder who made the play throws ahead of the lead runner who is not already scoring.

Humans: on defense, the stick moves the ball holder and the base buttons throw (the AI takes over after `human_throw_decision_window` idle). On offense, `runner_advance` / `runner_retreat` (E/Q, RB/LB) move all runners; a runner given an order is no longer steered by the AI. ThrowPlay and AiDefense were removed: there is one model of a play, and AI-vs-AI sims run the same LivePlay at `sim_play_step`.

Reason: Owner request. Live decisions and rundowns need position and time, not a fixed list of commitments.

Affected systems: new `baseball/running/live_play.gd`; BallInPlay (`start_live`, `resolve_ai`), RunnerResolver (read only), PlayOutcome (`doubled_off`), MatchDirector (one fixed-step loop for LiveFielding and LivePlay, intents, event callouts), MatchView (draws from LivePlay), input (`runner_advance`, `runner_retreat`), tuning, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Cutoff Men

Decision: A throw at least `cutoff_min_throw` (45 m) long from the fielder who made the play goes to a cutoff man. He lines up between the thrower and the target base, `cutoff_distance_from_base` (30 m) from it and at most halfway. He catches the ball and, after `cutoff_release_time` (0.3 s), relays to the original base. The AI cutoff re-decides: relay, throw elsewhere, or hold. A human's relay goes through unless they press another base while the ball is on its way in. Runners judge the ball through the cutoff, so a long throw home no longer hands trailing runners a free base. AI hold throws from the outfield use the cutoff, which then holds. Presentation picks the cutoff from a table (home: 3B or 1B by field side; third: SS; second: SS or 2B; first: 2B), and covers are reassigned around him.

Reason: Owner request. Removes the long-throw exploit, which had produced too many extra bases, and adds a defensive decision.

Affected systems: LivePlay (CUTOFF slot, routing, relay, redirect, ETAs), tuning, MatchView, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Asset Discovery Guide

Decision: `14_ASSET_SOURCES.md` is the approved discovery guide. It sets a tiered search order, per-source license expectations, what to reuse versus build ourselves, a baseball-specific search checklist and a selection score. `13_ASSET_MANIFEST.md` remains the record of what was actually obtained and under which terms; the guide approves nothing by itself. Quaternius packs must record whether the exact download was CC0 or the 2026 Quaternius Asset License.

Reason: Owner-provided guide; reduces search time and license risk.

Affected systems: asset pipeline, manifest, agent rules. No assets imported yet.

## 2026-10-20 - Per-Runner Orders

Decision: On offense, holding a base button (the same 1-4 / B Y X A used for throws) while pressing `runner_advance` / `runner_retreat` orders only the runner associated with that base. That is the runner standing on it, else the one heading to it, else the one who just left it (`LivePlay.runner_for_base`). With no base held, the order goes to every runner as before. Only runners who receive an order stop being AI-steered; a retreat order to a runner on a bag means "stay". The view rings the runner a held base would pick.

Reason: Owner request; reuses the existing base buttons so there are no new verbs to learn.

Affected systems: LivePlay (offense intents), MatchDirector, MatchView, playtest bot, README, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Procedural Placeholder Animation

Decision: Until the canonical rig exists (Phase 2), bodies are animated procedurally. `ActorView` has a small rig (body lean, bob, sideways sway, one arm with a glove) and poses: IDLE, RUN, TURN, CHASE, TAG, THROW, DOWN, SLIDE. `PlayAnimator` is a pure, testable presentation reader of `LivePlay`: it picks each runner's and the holder's pose from reversals, rundown events, holder movement, tag-out and throw events, and close plays at a bag. It also picks the actor who just threw. `MatchView` applies the poses, draws a held ball in the holder's glove, keeps out runners visible while they go down, and pulls the field camera in during a rundown. Nothing feeds back into the play.

Reason: Owner request for rundown animation; keeps "AnimationTree/state machines must not contain baseball rules" by making the animator a read-only consumer.

Affected systems: ActorView, new `presentation/match/play_animator.gd`, MatchView (pose pass, rundown camera), tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Batting Readability Pass (First Playtest)

Decision: After the owner's first playtest (MacBook: "it just slings balls one after another; no easy way to swing or see the bat"):
- **Pacing:** the AI pre-pitch is now 1.8 s, ending in a visible 0.7 s pitcher windup with the ball in his glove.
- **Bat:** the batter holds a visible bat in a stance and swings it on every swing.
- **Timing ring:** a ring on the plate closes as the pitch arrives and turns green at the plate. It sits at the zone centre, so it never reveals pitch location.
- **Camera:** the batting camera is now a raised telephoto from behind and right of the plate.
- **Rookie assist (default on, T):** pitches at 0.85 speed and a 1.3x timing window for a human batter only. It is applied through `PitchIntent.speed_scale` and `SwingIntent.window_scale`, so the rules stay authoritative.
- **Mac keys:** plain-key alternatives for the F-keys (` debug, R restart).

Reason: The first human test showed the hitting loop was unreadable. The brief's 2-minute goal ("understand how to pitch and hit") was failing.

Affected systems: MatchDirector (pacing, assist, prompts), MatchView (bat, windup, ring, camera), ActorView (catcher crouch), PitchIntent, SwingIntent, ContactResolver, PitchResolver, tuning, input map, README.

## 2026-10-20 - Hit Feedback and Aim Preview

Decision:
- **Contact feedback:** when the pitch reaches the plate, the callout names the contact tier (`ContactFeedback`: weak / OK / solid / sweet spot) and why: timing relative to that swing's window ("PERFECT", "a hair late", "late 60 ms"), the pitch location (middle, edge, chased) and, in play, exit velocity and launch angle.
- **Big callout:** a sweet-spot hit (quality >= 0.9) gets BARRELED!, or CRUSHED! on a power swing, and a burst at the plate. It stays rare per the UI brief.
- **Contact sound:** a bat crack synthesized in code (`ContactSound`: filtered noise, brighter for better contact). It uses no third-party audio, so the manifest stays empty.
- **Aim preview:** while batting, an arrow from the plate shows where the stick sends an on-time swing. Long and blue means lift, short and brown means a chop. The arrow does not include timing, which still pulls or pushes the ball.

Reason: Owner asked how to aim and how to find the sweet spot for homers; nothing on screen taught either.

Affected systems: new `ui/hud/contact_feedback.gd`, new `presentation/audio/contact_sound.gd`, MatchView (aim arrow, flash, sound), MatchDirector (feedback at contact), README, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Phase 0 Feel Pass: Pitch Tells, Fielding Assist, Contact Punch

Decision:
- **Pitch tells:** each `PitchFamilyDef` has a `tell_color` and `tell_trail` (ghost-ball count): fastball long blue, breaking ball short orange, changeup a short green stub. The trail scales with the ball's readability growth. Colour and length both differ, so the cue is not colour-only.
- **Rookie assist on defense:** the human fielder gets `assist_fielder_speed_scale` 1.12 and `assist_reach_bonus` 0.35 m. With the stick at rest he auto-routes to the landing spot (or just ahead of a grounder). Any stick input overrides it, so misplays are still possible.
- **Contact punch:** solid contact and better kicks the camera (brief FOV zoom plus shake, stronger on a barrel). This is presentation only and a candidate for the "reduced camera shake" accessibility option.

Reason: The Phase 0 report's remaining weak spots: no hitter-side read, human fielding held to AI-perfect routes, and flat contact.

Affected systems: PitchFamilyDef and pitch data, MatchView (trail, punch), LiveFielding (assist), MatchDirector, tuning, README, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Phase 0 Close-out: Errors, Failed Dives, Wall Caroms, Auto-switch, Hit Pause

Decision:
- **Errors are authoritative and seeded:** `LivePlay` rolls them with the match `MatchRng` (with no rng, the play is error-free, which keeps unit tests and old sims exact).
  - A ground ball can be bobbled (`bobble_chance` plus a per-m/s term).
  - Any throw can sail (`throw_error_chance` plus a per-meter term). It lands to one side and past the target, and the backup's retrieval time scales with how far it got away.
  - Standing runners, and runners mid-stride, read a misplay and take the next base if it is there.
- **Failed dives:** a dive that reaches the ball fails `dive_drop_chance` (30%) of the time, and the ball gets past the fielder. It is scored as a hit, not an error.
- **Scoring:** the batter safe because of a bobble or a wild throw to first is `reached_on_error`, which is not a hit. Errors are listed on `PlayOutcome.errors`.
- **Wall caroms:** a ball fielded after it came off the wall costs `wall_carom_delay` (0.8 s). Runners' reads see it through `ready_time`. This is the source of triples (0.7% of balls in play).
- **Rookie assist** halves error chances for a human fielder (`assist_error_scale`).
- **Auto-switch:** on a ball on the ground, control passes to a teammate once the human's fielder is 8 m or more away and the teammate is within half that distance. The manual switch stays.
- **Hit pause and follow camera (presentation only):**
  - The play clock holds 50 ms on solid contact and 110 ms on a barrel.
  - The field camera pulls back on deep plays and tilts up with the ball's height.

Reason: The remaining Phase 0 weak spots (no triples, no defensive failure, free dives, a human stranded by a grounder through the hole, flat contact). This closes Phase 0.

Affected systems: LivePlay, PlayOutcome, BallInPlay, AutoMatch, BallTrajectory, FieldingResolver, LiveFielding, MatchDirector, MatchView, tuning, sim report, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Phase 1: Gearheads vs Glitterati, Arcade Rules, Ratings, Subs

Decision:
- **Clubs (owner's pick):** the Rustwater Gearheads (away) and the Neon Heights Glitterati (home). They are data (`TeamDef`/`PlayerDef` resources) generated from `tools/generate_teams.gd`, with stable ids separate from display names.
- **Who's on First (owner request):** the Glitterati lineup is named after the routine's positions; the right fielder stays unnamed. The names are short phrases, and no dialogue from the routine is reproduced.
- **Arcade rules package (owner's pick, `MatchRules`):** 6 innings, universal DH, a 10-run mercy rule after 4 innings, a runner on second in extra innings, a 15 s pitch clock for human pitchers (an automatic ball), and no re-entry. `MatchState` reads its counts and endings from the rules. Passing an innings count gives classic rules, which old tests use.
- **Ratings** (0–100, 50 = average) scale existing mechanics through `Ratings`. They never add new dice, and average players change nothing. The spreads are tuning values.
- **AI personality** lives on `TeamDef`. The bullpen AI (`AiManager`) uses fatigue and a hook, plus a closer in save spots.
- **Substitutions:** the human can make a pitching change or use a pinch hitter between pitches. The AI manages the other club's pitching.
- **Balance:** the Glitterati win about 62% of AI-vs-AI games, which is accepted for now as the underdog story. The real fix is steals and bunts for the small-ball Gearheads.
- **Scoring:** a play with a runner forced out is a fielder's choice even if the batter takes an extra base on it.
- **Debug overlay:** it now starts hidden (` or F1 shows it).

Reason: Phase 1 vertical slice. The owner chose the teams and arcade rules, and asked for the Who's on First names.

Affected systems: data (teams, rules, defs), MatchState, MatchRoster, Ratings, AiBatter/AiPitcher/AiManager, PitchResolver, ContactResolver, FielderState, RunnerResolver, LivePlay, BallInPlay, AutoMatch, MatchDirector, MatchHud, MatchView, ActorView, input map, bot, sim report, tests.

## 2026-10-20 - Directional Throws

Decision: A fielder throws by holding a direction and pressing throw (Space / J / left click, RB on a gamepad).
- Directions follow the diamond as seen from behind home: right = first, up = second, left = third, down = home.
- A centered stick throws to the best play, which is the AI's choice.
- A green ring shows the aimed base while the fielder holds the ball.
- The 1–4 keys and the B / Y / X / A face buttons still throw straight to a base.
- Throws are now buffered before the catch too. The README always promised this, but a press before the catch used to be dropped.

Reason: Owner request: throw with WASD and the normal movement controls. Pointing and pressing is the standard arcade scheme, and it works the same on keyboard and gamepad.

Affected systems: ThrowAim (new), LivePlay (auto throw, buffering), MatchDirector, MatchView, input map, README, bot, tests.

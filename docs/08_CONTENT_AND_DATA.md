# CONTENT & DATA MODEL

## Stable IDs

Every player, team, stadium, trait, ability, equipment item, sponsor, event, and cosmetic uses a stable internal ID separate from display text.

## Player Definition

Identity: ID, name, nickname, number, handedness, positions, archetype.

Ratings: baseball skills, Toughness, Charisma.

Personality: risk appetite, aggression, showmanship, leadership, temperament.

Traits: passive/situational rules.

Signature content: pitch, Show Play, celebration, entrance.

Career: age/experience, contract, fatigue, injury state, fame, relationships, records.

Visual: body archetype, face preset, hair, uniform fit, gear, portrait recipe.

## Team Definition

ID, city/region, team name, colors, logo/mascot references, roster, strategy profile, Show philosophy, crowd culture, stadium, rivals, presentation package.

## Stadium Definition

Dimensions, wall geometry, surface, camera anchors, crowd capacity, attractions, lighting profile, audio profile, gameplay modifiers, fan-catch zones, performance tiers.

## Content Budget for First Vertical Slice

- 2 fictional teams
- 18-24 players per team can be represented in data, but only active gameplay roster needs polished visuals
- 1 stadium
- 4 pitch families
- 6-10 traits
- 4-6 Show Plays
- 2 collision options
- 6-8 celebration stingers
- 1 rivalry presentation package

## Naming & Legal Safety

Create original league/team/player names and branding. Inspirations guide tone and systems; do not recreate protected logos, uniforms, characters, exact UI, audio, or proprietary text/rules presentation.

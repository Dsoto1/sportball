# Phase 1 Report: Baseball Vertical Slice

Date: 2026-10-20. Scope per `09_MVP_ROADMAP.md`: two teams, a full
inning/outs/count/bases/scoring game, substitutions, basic AI and a
6-inning exhibition, on free/CC0 placeholders (still all primitives).

## What exists

- **Two clubs** (`data/teams/*.tres`, generated by `tools/generate_teams.gd`):
  the **Rustwater Gearheads** (scrappy small ball, gloves and speed) at the
  **Neon Heights Glitterati** (glamour and power). Each has 17 players
  (9 starters, 3 bench, 5 pitchers), colors, a mascot, a stadium, a rival,
  and an AI personality.
- **Who's on First.** The Glitterati lineup is the vaudeville routine:
  Who (1B), What (2B), I Don't Know (3B), Why (LF), Because (CF), Tomorrow
  (P), Today (C), I Don't Care (SS). Nobody has ever named the right
  fielder. The broadcast calls out "WHO'S ON FIRST" when he gets there.
  These are short phrases used as names; none of the routine's dialogue is
  reproduced.
- **Arcade rules** (`data/rules/arcade.tres`, `MatchRules`): 6 innings,
  universal DH, a mercy rule at 10 runs after 4 innings, a runner on second
  in extra innings, a 15 s pitch clock for human pitchers (a violation is
  an automatic ball), and no re-entry.
- **Ratings matter, gently.** `Ratings` maps 0–100 ratings onto
  play:
  - Pitchers: velocity, control, stuff, and stamina-driven fatigue.
  - Batters: contact (timing window), power (exit velocity), eye (the AI's
    read and chase), and handedness (lefties pull to right field and stand
    in the other box).
  - Fielders: speed, glove (reach, first step, bobbles) and arm (throw
    speed, wild throws).
  - Runners: speed.

  League-average ratings (50) change nothing, so old tests and placeholder
  matches are exact.
- **AI personalities** per club: swing aggression, chase rate, power-swing
  rate, runner aggression, how much pitchers nibble, and the bullpen hook.
  `AiManager` pulls tired pitchers and brings in the closer to protect a
  lead of 3 or less in the last inning.
- **Substitutions** in `MatchState`: a pitching change or pinch hitter
  (P / gamepad Y between pitches), pinch runners, and no re-entry. Pitch
  counts are kept per pitcher. R/H/E are tracked per team.
- **Exhibition flow:**
  1. A pregame matchup card where you pick your club.
  2. A team-colored scoreboard with H and E, the batter (number, name,
     bats, nickname) and the pitcher's pitch count.
  3. Jersey numbers over fielders and names over runners.
  4. Team-colored uniforms with a cap and belt in each club's second color.
  5. A pitch-clock countdown.
  6. A final card with the box score (and "MERCY RULE" when it applies).

The teams play the way they are written: the Glitterati hit about 60% more
home runs and make more errors, and the Gearheads make fewer errors.

## Weak spots

1. **Balance: addressed with steals and bunts (2026-10-21).** In 400
   matches the Gearheads win 45.5% (it was 38%). They steal 1.5 bases per
   game at 81% and bunt about 1.7 times per game. The Glitterati remain the
   slight favorite with their power. With pickoffs (2.7 throws over and
   0.36 pickoffs per game) and slide steps, the Gearheads win 44.5% (800
   matches). See DECISIONS.
2. **Pitch clock only for human pitchers.** The AI never runs it down, and
   there is no batter-side clock.
3. **No in-game lineup card or defensive substitutions.** You can change
   the pitcher and pinch hit, but not move fielders or double-switch.
4. **Placeholder look.** Uniform colors, numbers and caps are readable, but
   these are still capsules. Phase 2 is the art pass, and readability
   assets need your approval of proposed CC0 sources.

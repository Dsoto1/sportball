# GAME VISION

## Working Description

A stylized retro-futurist arcade baseball game about building a team that can win the game and win the crowd. Players pitch, hit, field, run, steal, collide, improvise trick plays, develop stars, cultivate rivalries, and grow a small club into the greatest sports show in the world.

## Core Fantasy

You are not simply controlling nine baseball players. You are creating a legendary entertainment franchise with recognizable stars, signature plays, hated rivals, unforgettable stadiums, and stories that persist across seasons.

## Four Design Pillars

### 1. Baseball First

The base game remains recognizable: pitching, batting, outs, bases, steals, double plays, substitutions, defensive positioning, and situational decisions. Arcade simplification is welcome; baseball readability is mandatory.

### 2. Every Player Is a Character

Silhouette, animation, ratings, traits, temperament, relationships, fame, history, signature tricks, celebrations, and rivalries make players memorable beyond statistics.

### 3. The Crowd Is a System

The audience reacts to quality baseball and entertainment. The team can take risks for Show, but reckless spectacle can cost runs and games.

### 4. No Dead Air

Transitions are quick. Players, mascots, crowds, scoreboards, music, announcers, and small ambient behaviors keep the world alive between pitches without slowing input.

## Tone

Joyful competitive chaos. Confident, colorful, theatrical, mischievous, occasionally absurd. Violence is sports-cartoon slapstick. The world takes its league seriously even when the league is ridiculous.

## Player Experience Goals

- Within 2 minutes: understand how to pitch and hit.
- Within 10 minutes: attempt a risky Show Play on purpose.
- Within 30 minutes: have a favorite player.
- Within a season: remember a rivalry or emergent story.
- Long term: feel ownership over a franchise's identity and history.

## Anti-Goals

This is not a photoreal MLB simulator, pure party minigame collection, fighting game wearing baseball uniforms, generic cyberpunk sports game, card battler with baseball dressing, or management spreadsheet where games are secondary.
